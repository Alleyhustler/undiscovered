Rabi En Rose is a character from the multimedia franchise Di Gi Charat, this page of my website is dedicated to her!

About

Rabi~En~Rose is an aspiring idol and Dejiko's rival. She tends to be competitive in her interactions with Dejiko, although in the manga it is hinted that she actually wants to be her friend. Rabi En Rose's actual name is Hikaru Usada, although she hates being referred to by that name. She is 14 years old and attends high school when not working at GAMERS.
