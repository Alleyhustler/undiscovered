Introduction

Screen Name(s): Toast, Toastchan

Gender: Female

Nice to meet you. I use many different names online, but on my website I go by Toastchan or just Toast. I like a lot of things and I like creating things and that is what this website is dedicated to.

I think that one of the most wonderful things about the internet is that you don't know who anyone really is. All you have to go from is the information that they have voluntarily displayed. You do not know a person's sex, race, class, etc. unless they tell you.

I want to express myself without the burden of 'identity'.
