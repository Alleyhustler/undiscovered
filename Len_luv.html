Welcome to the Kagamine Len room!

Kagamine Len is one of my favorite vocaloids... this is a page about him!

Who is Kagamine Len?

Kagamine Len is a voicebank released for the Vocaloid2 vocal synthesis software alongside Kagamine Rin on December 27 2007 by Crypton Future Media. His voice provider is Asami Shimoda. Kagamine Len's original character art was designed and drawn by Japanese illustrator, KEI however art for subsequent versions has been done by different artists.

Technical information

Age: 14

Height: 156cm (5' 1")

Weight: 47kg (103 lbs)

Color: Yellow

Why do you like him so much?

I like the range and versatility of his voice that can range from cutesy and childlike to cool and mature. I also think that his vocals and appearance both complement and contrast well with Kaito, another one of my favorite vocaloids. Even though his voicebank has been improved on greatly over the years I just have a soft spot for v2 Len, I find the more robotic sound of his initial voicebank very charming.

What is your favorite song featuring Len?

My number one favorite Len song has to be Crystal Mic by HzEdge/Crystal-P. It is an old and not very well known song by a rather obscure producer (Crystal-P is better known for Erase or zero) however I still love the song after first hearing it years ago. The lyrics are very pure and bittersweet and they never grow old to me.
